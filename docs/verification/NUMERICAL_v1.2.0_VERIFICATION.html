What this does not authorize

No complete multi-decade production case was executed for this release. Eight complete climate files still have subzero air forcing and fail the existing conservative ice-free scope check. QA passing and an input freeze do not override that separate physical limitation. Do not silently delete cold hours, clamp temperatures, omit cases, or label local tests as paper results.

1. The initialization hypothesis did not solve the failures

The user's supplied spinup.npz matched its metadata checksum and old code/configuration identity. Testing that state in the legacy method, with exact nested cell splitting for a common-state 400-cell comparison, left the four difficult windows above 5%. That was an initialization-sensitivity diagnostic, not independent fine-mesh spin-up or the actual field decades into a future run. The hypothesis was rejected; no QA gate was relaxed or switched to a more favourable initial state.

2. The actual numerical revision

The internal liquid-flow face coefficient now uses the integral of the original conductivity over the pressure interval between the two adjacent cells, divided by that pressure difference. It replaces the legacy harmonic endpoint average. For the selected piecewise-linear pC(theta) and log10 K(theta) functions, that integral is evaluated analytically on the source knots. The same mean multiplies pressure and gravity terms, and flux is still shared conservatively by both cells.

The brick source bytes, material/scalar values, climate files, water/salt supply, exposed-face drainage assumption, 200-cell setting, BDF tolerances, 5% gate, metric floors, 0.10 m vertical bands and generic QA initial conditions are unchanged. The method and implementation files are fingerprinted; old certificates cannot certify this revision. This is not a new measured material law, a DELPHIN-equivalent validation, or a new damage/phase model.
